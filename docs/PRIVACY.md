# Privacy

AsterMark works entirely on your Mac.

- **Your photos never leave your computer.** AsterMark has no network access in its sandbox (it has no network entitlement) and sends nothing anywhere.
- **Originals are never modified.** AsterMark only reads the photos in the folders you open, and writes new files only to the export folders you choose.
- **What's stored, and where:** the watermark graphics you import, your layouts, outputs and album edits, kept in AsterMark's own container (`~/Library/Containers/<bundle id>/Data/Library/Application Support/AsterMark`). The settings you enter (canvas background, photographer name, copyright and contact details) are kept in the app's preferences. Nothing is stored in your photo folders.
- **Metadata:** exports follow each output's metadata setting. The Instagram and Facebook outputs remove GPS location and camera serial numbers by default. Your name, copyright and contact details (Settings ▸ Photographer) are added to exports only when the output's "Add my name and copyright" option is on.
- **Diagnostics:** macOS may deliver performance and crash reports to AsterMark through MetricKit. They're saved locally (Help ▸ Show Diagnostics Folder) and never uploaded; you can attach them to a bug report if you choose.
- **Links:** Help ▸ AsterMark on GitHub and Report a Problem open pages in your web browser. The app itself sends nothing.
